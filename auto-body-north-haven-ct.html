Finding us

Where the shop actually is.

Rebeschi Drive sits in the commercial block east of Washington Avenue, close enough to Universal Drive that most people in town have driven past the turn without noticing it.

From I-91, take exit 12 and head toward Washington Avenue. From the Universal Drive shopping area, Rebeschi Drive is a short run behind the retail strip. If you are coming down Route 5 from Wallingford, stay south past Devine Street and watch for the turn on your left.

Coming from Route 22 or the Montowese side of town, cut across toward Sackett Point Road and you will land in the same industrial pocket. There is room to leave a vehicle, and we can receive a tow directly.

If your car is not safe to drive, call before you agree to a tow destination. Vehicles routed to a storage lot start accruing daily fees immediately, and getting one released later is slower and more expensive than sending it here in the first place.

You choose the shop that repairs your vehicle. An insurance company can recommend one, but the decision is yours to make.

Questions

North Haven FAQs.

Where exactly are you in North Haven?

78 Rebeschi Drive, North Haven, CT 06473. It is in the commercial block east of Washington Avenue, near the Universal Drive area. From I-91 the closest approach is exit 12.

Do I have to use the shop my insurance company recommends?

No. In Connecticut you choose the repair facility. A carrier can suggest a shop in its network, but it cannot require you to use one. Tell your adjuster you are having the vehicle repaired at Exclusive Auto Lab and the claim gets processed here.

My car is not drivable. What should I do first?

Call the shop before the vehicle is towed anywhere. Once a car goes to a storage lot it starts accruing daily fees, and releasing it takes paperwork and time. We can help coordinate a tow that comes straight here instead.

Do you do customization work, or only collision?

Both, in the same building. Paint protection film, ceramic coating, window and lighting tint, vinyl wraps, chrome delete, paint correction, wheel and caliper work and custom exterior builds are all handled in house alongside the collision side.

Can I get PPF or coating on a brand-new car?

Yes, and that is the best time for it. Film and coating go on cleanest before the paint has picked up chips and swirls. If a vehicle already has some wear, paint correction first will noticeably improve the result.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the damage, parts availability and how fast the claim is approved. Cosmetic work can be a few days. Structural repairs, back-ordered parts and supplement approvals take longer. You get a realistic timeline after teardown, not a guess before it.

Do you work on newer vehicles with sensors and cameras?

Yes. Bumpers, mirrors and windshields on newer vehicles house parking sensors, radar and camera modules. Those are written into the repair plan so components go back correctly rather than being left disconnected.
