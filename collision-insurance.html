After an Accident

Here is what to do next.

A collision is stressful. These four steps help protect your safety, your vehicle and your ability to choose the repair shop you trust.

01

Stay Safe

Move to a safe location when possible, check for injuries and contact emergency services when needed.

02

Document the Scene

Take photos of the vehicles, damage, license plates and surroundings. Exchange contact and insurance information.

03

Call Exclusive Auto Lab

Before authorizing repairs, call or text us. We can discuss towing, the claim and the best next step for your vehicle.

04

We Handle the Repair

We inspect the damage, develop the repair plan, communicate throughout the process and keep you updated until delivery.
