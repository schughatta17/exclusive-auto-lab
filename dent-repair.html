Overview

What paintless dent repair actually does.

PDR uses specialized rods and tools to massage a dent out from the back side of the panel, plus glue-pull tabs on the outside where there is no access behind. The paint never comes off.

The reason PDR matters is that factory paint is better than anything applied afterward. It was baked onto bare metal in a controlled plant environment before the car was assembled. Any refinish, no matter how well done, is a repair. Keeping the original finish keeps the panel original, which matters for resale and for anyone checking a vehicle history.

It is also faster and costs less. There is no sanding, priming, spraying, curing or blending, so a door ding that would take days as a refinish job can often be handled far more quickly.

The trade-off is that PDR has limits. It works when the paint is unbroken and the metal has not been stretched past the point where it will return to shape. Sharp creases, torn metal, dents on a panel edge, and damage where the paint has already cracked are conventional repair jobs.

We tell you honestly which one your panel needs. Forcing PDR on a dent that is beyond it produces a panel that looks almost right, which is worse than doing it properly the first time.

Worth knowing

When PDR works, and when it does not.

PDR is the right call when the paint is unbroken, the metal is not stretched, and there is access behind the panel or a workable surface for glue pulls. It is the wrong call when the paint has cracked, the metal has torn, the dent sits on a panel edge, or the damage is deep and sharp. We look at the panel and tell you which it is before quoting, rather than starting one and switching to the other.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is paintless dent repair?

PDR is a technique for pushing a dent back out from behind the panel using specialized rods, or pulling it from the outside with glue tabs. No sanding, filler, primer or paint is involved, so the factory finish stays exactly as it was.

Is PDR cheaper than traditional dent repair?

Usually yes, and it is faster. There is no prep, spray, cure or blend stage, so the labor is a fraction of a conventional repair. The savings are largest on multiple small dents like hail damage.

Can every dent be fixed with PDR?

No. PDR requires the paint to be unbroken and the metal not stretched past its ability to return to shape. Cracked paint, torn metal, very sharp creases and dents right on a panel edge need conventional repair and refinishing.

Will paintless dent repair damage my paint?

Done correctly on a suitable dent, no. The whole point is that the paint is never touched. That said, if the paint is already cracked or has been previously repainted and is poorly bonded, working the metal can lift it. We check paint condition first.

Does insurance cover hail damage repair?

Hail is typically handled as a comprehensive claim rather than collision, though it depends on your policy. Carriers generally prefer PDR for hail because it costs less than refinishing multiple panels. We document the damage and work the claim with you.

How long does dent repair take?

A single door ding is often same-day. Multiple dents, hail damage across several panels, or anything needing conventional repair and refinishing takes longer. We give you a timeline after seeing the vehicle.

Should I fix a small dent or leave it?

If the paint is intact, a dent is cosmetic and there is no urgency beyond appearance and resale. If the paint has cracked or chipped, bare metal is exposed, and Connecticut road salt will start rust there within a season or two. Cracked paint moves it from cosmetic to worth handling.
